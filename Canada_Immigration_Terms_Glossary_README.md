# Canada Immigration Terms Glossary

## Overview

This document is a plain-English glossary of the key terms used in the Canada immigration and respiratory therapy licensing research. It is designed to make technical or legal immigration language easier to understand for someone planning a move from India to Canada.

## Scope

The glossary covers:

- Canadian government and immigration law terms
- Express Entry and CRS terminology
- NOC and TEER classification concepts
- consultant regulator and verification language
- respiratory therapy licensing terms
- credential evaluation and language test terminology
- provincial and territory references relevant to healthcare migration

## Why this file matters

The immigration landscape includes many terms that are easy to confuse. This file helps clarify the difference between:

- IRCC, CIC, and CICC
- NOC 32103 vs LPN categories
- RCIC vs consultant vs lawyer
- Express Entry vs provincial nominee programs
- CRTO, CRTA, NSRRT, and other regulating bodies

## Best use

Use this guide alongside the main research report and the consultant vetting checklist to understand what a consultant, regulator, or immigration resource is referring to.

## Key themes

- Express Entry basics and ranking points
- Permanent residency terminology and required documentation
- Licensing frameworks for respiratory therapists in Canada
- Verification of regulated advisors
- Canadian healthcare pathway terminology
