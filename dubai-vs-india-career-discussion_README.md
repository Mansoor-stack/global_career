# Dubai vs India Career Discussion

## Overview

This document is a comparison of career and lifestyle outcomes in Dubai versus India for two scenarios:

- a respiratory therapist at the early-to-mid career stage
- a data engineer with around 10 years of professional experience

## Main themes

- salary comparisons between India and Dubai
- purchasing power and cost-of-living adjustments
- savings opportunities under different housing and family scenarios
- licensing and work conditions in the UAE
- the practical effect of family and spouse income on couple-level decision making

## Why this file matters

The document helps structure a realistic decision around whether a move to Dubai is worth it. It avoids simplistic salary comparisons and instead examines:

- living standards after adjusting for local prices
- savings potential in the Gulf versus India
- family affordability and housing requirements
- licensing and immigration barriers in UAE healthcare roles
- whether the couple would be meaningfully better off together in Dubai

## Key conclusions

- For a respiratory therapist, the lifestyle difference is not dramatic once cost of living is considered.
- The larger upside is savings, especially when housing is provided or shared.
- For a data engineer, Dubai can offer strong salary potential and better combined household savings, but the improvement depends on job quality, family costs, and housing terms.
- The move makes more sense as a couple strategy than as a pure lifestyle upgrade.
