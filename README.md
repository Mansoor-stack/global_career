# Global Career Repository

This repository brings together research notes and planning documents related to immigration decisions, career comparison, and consultant evaluation.

It is designed to help a family understand:
- the Canada immigration process
- respiratory therapy licensing in Canada
- how to vet consultants before paying
- whether Dubai or India is a better fit for the couple's goals

## Files in this repository

### 1) `Canada_Immigration_Terms_Glossary.md`
This document explains the main immigration and healthcare terms in plain English.

Use it when you want simple definitions for terms such as:
- Express Entry
- CRS
- NOC / TEER
- PR / ITA
- RCIC / CICC
- CRTO / CRTA / NSRRT
- IELTS / CLB / NCLC

Why it matters:
- It makes the larger immigration reports easier to understand.
- It helps you avoid confusion between legal, licensing, and immigration terminology.

### 2) `Canada_RT_Immigration_Research_Report.md`
This is the main research document for the Canada move.

It covers:
- respiratory therapy licensing in Canada
- the practical steps for a foreign-trained RT from India
- Express Entry and provincial nominee pathways
- province-wise opportunities and salary expectations
- realistic timeline and budget
- consultant choices and warning signs
- official sources to verify before taking action

Why it matters:
- It gives the full picture of what is involved in moving to Canada as a respiratory therapist.
- It helps separate immigration planning from licensing, which are often mixed up.

### 3) `Consultant_Vetting_Checklist.md`
This file is a practical checklist to review any immigration consultant, lawyer, or agency before paying anything.

It includes:
- questions to ask before hiring anyone
- how to verify a consultant's license
- red flags and scam warning signs
- contract and refund checks
- how to compare multiple options fairly

Why it matters:
- It helps you avoid fake promises, misleading advice, and unnecessary fees.
- It is the best document to use before speaking with or paying any advisor.

### 4) `dubai-vs-india-career-discussion.md`
This document compares the practical value of working in Dubai versus India for the couple.

It covers:
- respiratory therapist salary comparison
- cost of living and purchasing power adjustment
- savings potential in each location
- data engineering job market and salary outlook
- family and lifestyle trade-offs
- visa and timing considerations

Why it matters:
- It helps compare real-life living and earning conditions, not just headline salaries.
- It is useful when deciding whether Dubai is worth the move for a couple.
