# Consultant Vetting Checklist

## Overview

This checklist is a practical tool for screening immigration consultants, lawyers, and recruitment agencies before any payment is made. It is designed for a respiratory therapist from India planning a move to Canada.

## Purpose

The checklist aims to help prevent fraud, avoid unnecessary fees, and identify consultants who are not qualified or are offering misleading services.

## Core sections

- facts to know before any consultation
- questions to ask during a consultation
- red flags and safety rules
- guidance on verifying licensing and representative status
- comparison scorecard for evaluating multiple firms
- notes template for recording consultant calls

## Why this file matters

This document helps the user distinguish between legitimate immigration professionals and bad actors. It addresses critical topics such as:

- verifying whether a consultant is truly licensed
- checking whether they are named on the correct representative forms
- avoiding fake job offers or guaranteed PR promises
- understanding the real role of regulators and the separation of licensing from immigration advice
- checking India-side recruiting agencies against proper registration rules

## Best practices highlighted

- Speak to the licensed person before paying.
- Verify consultants through the official register.
- Get a written service agreement before making payment.
- Watch for staged-fee structures and refund wording.
- Avoid any promise of guaranteed visa outcomes or job offers.
