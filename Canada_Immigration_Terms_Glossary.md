# Glossary: Canada Immigration & Respiratory Therapy Licensing Terms

**Prepared for:** Mansoor Ahmed & family
**Compiled:** 28 October 2026
**Purpose:** plain-English meanings of every term used in the research report and consultant checklist.

> Entries marked **🔧 Clarified** were adjusted from the original breakdown for accuracy. Entries marked **➕ Added** are extra terms that came up in the research.

---

## 1. Canadian Government & Immigration Law

| Term | Meaning | Why it matters to you |
|---|---|---|
| **IRCC**: Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada | The federal government department that manages visas, permanent residency (PR), citizenship, study permits, work permits and immigration applications. | Makes all final immigration decisions. No consultant can influence the outcome. |
| **CIC**: Citizenship and Immigration Canada | The former name of IRCC, used until 2015. | Old articles and forms may still say "CIC". |
| **CIC News** | A Canadian immigration news website (run by Cohen Immigration Law / CanadaVisa) that publishes updates, rule changes and draw results. | Useful for tracking Express Entry draws and rule changes. |
| **IRPA**: Immigration and Refugee Protection Act | The main Canadian immigration law that sets rules, eligibility, rights and legal procedures. | **Section 91** says only RCICs, Canadian lawyers and Quebec notaries can charge for immigration advice. |
| **PR**: Permanent Residency **🔧 Clarified** | Status that lets someone live, work and study anywhere in Canada indefinitely without being a citizen. PR holders must live in Canada at least **2 of every 5 years** to keep it. | The goal of this whole plan. |
| **PNP**: Provincial Nominee Programs | Immigration programs run by individual provinces to select immigrants for local job and labour needs. | A provincial nomination adds **+600 CRS points**, almost guaranteeing an invitation. |
| **SINP** ➕ Added | Saskatchewan Immigrant Nominee Program. | Runs the **Health Talent Pathway** (job offer from a SK health employer). |
| **OINP** ➕ Added | Ontario Immigrant Nominee Program. | Old streams were repealed 30 May 2026; the new **Ontario Workforce Priority Stream** needs an Ontario job offer. |
| **AAIP** ➕ Added | Alberta Advantage Immigration Program. | Runs the **Dedicated Health Care Pathway** (check if RTs are eligible). |
| **LMIA**: Labour Market Impact Assessment **🔧 Clarified** | A document a Canadian employer gets to show it may hire a foreign worker because no suitable Canadian worker was available. | From **3 Nov 2026**, Express Entry job-offer points **no longer require an LMIA** for qualifying high-wage offers. |
| **CBSA** ➕ Added | Canada Border Services Agency: border control and enforcement. | One recommended consultancy (Canada Abroad) was founded by a former CBSA officer. |

## 2. Express Entry & Points System

| Term | Meaning | Why it matters to you |
|---|---|---|
| **Express Entry (EE)** ➕ Added | Canada's online system for managing skilled-worker PR applications. | The main route for your wife. |
| **FSW**: Federal Skilled Worker Program ➕ Added | The Express Entry program for skilled workers with foreign (non-Canadian) experience. | Her eligible program. It needs 1 year of continuous skilled work experience. |
| **CEC**: Canadian Experience Class ➕ Added | Express Entry program for people with Canadian work experience. | Not applicable now. |
| **CRS**: Comprehensive Ranking System | The points system that ranks Express Entry candidates (age, education, language, experience, spouse factors, etc.). | 2026 healthcare cut-offs were **467–475**. |
| **ITA**: Invitation to Apply (ITA stage) | The step where IRCC officially invites a candidate to submit a full PR application. | **No Canadian licence is needed at this stage.** You then have 60 days to apply. |
| **Category-based draws** ➕ Added | Express Entry rounds that target specific groups (healthcare, French speakers, trades, etc.). | Her occupation qualifies for **healthcare** draws after **12 months** of experience. |
| **STEM Category** **🔧 Clarified** | Science, Technology, Engineering and Mathematics occupations. It was once a targeted Express Entry category. | It has been **dormant for 30+ months**, so Mansoor's tech profile doesn't get special draws. That's why the wife should be the principal applicant. |
| **High-wage occupation list** ➕ Added | IRCC list of 186 occupations whose job offers earn extra CRS points from 3 Nov 2026. | **NOC 32103 is on it.** A genuine offer = 50/70/100 points. |
| **Principal applicant** ➕ Added | The main person on the application whose profile is scored. | Should be your wife (healthcare draws). |
| **Settlement funds / proof of funds** ➕ Added | Money you must show to support yourselves on arrival (not a fee). | About CAD 19,000 for a family of 2 (2025 figure; check current). |
| **RPRF** ➕ Added | Right of Permanent Residence Fee: CAD 600 per adult, refunded if refused. | Part of government costs (plus CAD 990 processing per adult). |
| **eAPR** ➕ Added | Electronic Application for Permanent Residence, submitted after an ITA. | The full PR application stage. |
| **COPR** ➕ Added | Confirmation of Permanent Residence: the approval document issued before landing. | Verify it in your own IRCC account (forgeries exist). |

## 3. Job Classification

| Term | Meaning | Why it matters to you |
|---|---|---|
| **NOC Code**: National Occupational Classification | Canada's official job classification system, used for immigration and work permits. | Her code is **32103**. Duties in her employment letter must match it. |
| **TEER** ➕ Added | Training, Education, Experience and Responsibilities: the skill-level category inside each NOC code (0–5). | RT is **TEER 2**. |
| **LPN**: Licensed Practical Nurse | A nurse providing basic care under supervision (NOC **32101**). | One consultant website wrongly listed RTs under 32101. That's a sign of carelessness. |

## 4. Consultant Regulation & Verification

| Term | Meaning | Why it matters to you |
|---|---|---|
| **RCIC**: Regulated Canadian Immigration Consultant | A CICC-licensed professional legally authorized to give paid Canadian immigration advice and representation. | Always get their name + **R-number** and verify it. |
| **CICC**: College of Immigration and Citizenship Consultants | The official federal regulator that licenses and disciplines immigration consultants (replaced ICCRC in Nov 2021). | Where you verify consultants and file complaints. |
| **ICCRC** ➕ Added | Immigration Consultants of Canada Regulatory Council: the **old** regulator, replaced by CICC in 2021. | Firms still saying "ICCRC-registered" are using outdated wording, a carelessness signal. |
| **CICC Register / Public Register** | Official online database showing whether a consultant is licensed, in good standing, and any discipline history. | **register.college-ic.ca**: check before paying anything. |
| **Licensee** | A person licensed by the CICC to give paid immigration advice. | Only **individuals** are licensed, not companies. |
| **Ghost consultant** ➕ Added | An unlicensed person who gives paid advice but hides from IRCC by not being named on forms. | Illegal. Your application can be returned or refused. |
| **IMM 5476**: Use of a Representative form | The IRCC form that authorizes a consultant, lawyer or representative to act on your behalf. | The licensed person who handles your file **must** be named here. |
| **Law society** ➕ Added | Provincial body that licenses lawyers (e.g., Law Society of Ontario, Law Society of BC, Barreau du Québec). | Where you verify immigration lawyers. |
| **Paralegals** **🔧 Clarified** | Legal professionals who help lawyers with paperwork and case preparation. In **Ontario**, licensed paralegals can also be authorized representatives. | Most of your day-to-day contact at law firms may be with paralegals. |
| **Quebec Notary** | A licensed legal professional in Quebec (Chambre des notaires du Québec), authorized to act as an immigration representative. | One of the three groups allowed to charge for advice. |
| **CAPIC** ➕ Added | Canadian Association of Professional Immigration Consultants: a voluntary professional association, **not** a regulator. | Membership is a plus, but it doesn't replace CICC verification. |
| **Contingency billing** ➕ Added | Charging fees only if the application succeeds. | **Not allowed** for RCICs. "Pay only if approved" is a red flag. |
| **Service agreement / retainer** ➕ Added | The written contract stating scope, fees, milestones and refund policy. | Must be signed **before** any payment. |
| **Free Assessment Form** | An initial questionnaire firms use to check your eligibility before offering services. | Useful, but often a sales funnel. |
| **Flat-Fee Quote** | A fixed total price given upfront instead of hourly billing. | The normal model for Express Entry, usually paid in stages. |
| **HST** ➕ Added | Harmonized Sales Tax (13% in Ontario). | Usually **not charged** to clients outside Canada. |

## 5. Respiratory Therapy Licensing & Healthcare

| Term | Meaning | Why it matters to you |
|---|---|---|
| **Regulator Side / Regulator Sites** **🔧 Clarified** | In this research it means **two kinds** of official bodies: (1) **professional regulators** that license RTs (CRTO, CRTA, NSRRT, etc.), and (2) **consultant regulators** (CICC, law societies). | Licensing is decided by the RT regulators; consultant legitimacy by CICC/law societies. |
| **CRTO**: College of Respiratory Therapists of Ontario | Ontario's RT regulator. It also runs the competency assessment for NS, NB, NL and MB. | Her licensing journey will most likely start here. |
| **CRTA** ➕ Added | College of Respiratory Therapists of Alberta. | Alberta's RT regulator. |
| **NSRRT / NSCRT** ➕ Added | Nova Scotia's RT regulator (uses the CRTO assessment). | Relevant if you target Nova Scotia. |
| **MARRT / NBART / NLCHP** ➕ Added | RT regulators for Manitoba, New Brunswick, and Newfoundland & Labrador. | All refer foreign-trained RTs to the CRTO assessment. |
| **OPIQ** ➕ Added | Ordre professionnel des inhalothérapeutes du Québec: Quebec's RT regulator. | Requires French. |
| **IERT** ➕ Added | Internationally Educated Respiratory Therapist. | The term regulators use for her category. |
| **CSA**: Clinical Skills Assessment ➕ Added | CRTO's in-person simulated clinical exam in **Toronto**. | Costs **CAD 5,000**. The biggest licensing hurdle. |
| **SART** ➕ Added | Self-assessment readiness tool for foreign-trained RTs. | Free first step to gauge readiness. |
| **CSRT**: Canadian Society of Respiratory Therapists | The national professional association for RTs. It also issues the RRT credential in **unregulated** areas (BC and the territories). | Needed if working in BC. |
| **RRT**: Registered Respiratory Therapist **🔧 Clarified** | The professional title for a fully qualified RT. In regulated provinces it comes with provincial registration; in BC/territories it's granted by the CSRT. | Her target credential. |
| **CBRC / HPTC** **🔧 Clarified** | **CBRC** (Canadian Board for Respiratory Care) was the former name of the national exam body. It is now **HPTC (Health Professionals Testing Canada)**, which runs the national RT entry-to-practice exam. | She must pass the HPTC exam after the assessment. |
| **Gap / bridging training** ➕ Added | Extra education needed if the CRTO finds her training doesn't meet Canadian competencies. | Significant gaps may require a full Canadian RT program. |
| **Health Match BC** | A free, BC-government-funded service that helps healthcare professionals find jobs and relocate to BC. | Free, so register before paying anyone. |
| **SHRA / SHA** ➕ Added | Saskatchewan Healthcare Recruitment Agency / Saskatchewan Health Authority. | Run active international healthcare recruitment. |
| **Saskatchewan EOI** **🔧 Clarified** | Two things: (1) SINP's general **Expression of Interest** profile system, and (2) the separate free **International Healthcare Worker EOI pool**, where SK health employers can find and contact you. | Submit to the healthcare worker pool; it's free. |
| **IEHPs-CAN** ➕ Added | A mentorship program for internationally educated health professionals in Canada. | Useful **after** landing. |

## 6. Credential Evaluation & Language Tests

| Term | Meaning | Why it matters to you |
|---|---|---|
| **ECA**: Educational Credential Assessment ➕ Added | A report showing the Canadian equivalent of foreign degrees, required for Express Entry points. | Get it for **both** spouses (her MSc earns master's points). |
| **WES**: World Education Services | Evaluates foreign education for immigration and professional licensing. | She needs **two** reports: an ECA (IRCC) and a **course-by-course** report (CRTO). |
| **ICAS**: International Credential Assessment Service of Canada | Another credential evaluation organization. | Accepted alternative to WES for CRTO. |
| **ICES**: International Credential Evaluation Service | A credential evaluation service based in British Columbia (BCIT). | Accepted alternative to WES for CRTO. |
| **IELTS / CELPIP / PTE Core** ➕ Added | IRCC-approved English tests. For PR you need the **General Training** version of IELTS. | Both spouses should test; higher scores add CRS points. |
| **CLB / NCLC** ➕ Added | Canadian Language Benchmarks (English) / Niveaux de compétence linguistique canadiens (French). | CLB 9–10 boosts CRS. French NCLC 7 adds 50 points. |
| **TEF / TCF** ➕ Added | IRCC-approved French tests. | French is the biggest CRS lever. |

## 7. Canadian Provinces & Territories

| Code | Province | RT regulation / relevance |
|---|---|---|
| **ON** | Ontario | Most populous (Toronto, Ottawa). CRTO; largest RT job market. |
| **AB** | Alberta | Western province. CRTA; high wages. |
| **SK** | Saskatchewan | Prairie province. Health Talent Pathway; active recruitment. |
| **MB** | Manitoba | Central province. MARRT, uses the CRTO assessment. |
| **QC** | Quebec | French-speaking, with its own immigration system. OPIQ; French needed. |
| **NB** | New Brunswick | Eastern province. NBART, uses the CRTO assessment. |
| **NS** | Nova Scotia | Coastal eastern province. NSRRT; EE stream with no job offer. |
| **NL** | Newfoundland & Labrador | Easternmost province. NLCHP, uses the CRTO assessment. |
| **PEI** | Prince Edward Island | Smallest province. Regulated. |
| **BC** | British Columbia | Western province (Vancouver). Unregulated; CSRT credential; Health Match BC. |
| **YT / NT / NU** ➕ Added | Yukon, Northwest Territories, Nunavut | Unregulated; CSRT credential. |

## 8. Research & Review Platforms

| Term | Meaning | How to use it |
|---|---|---|
| **Trustpilot** | Public review platform for companies. | Sort by **lowest + newest**. Note any "review solicitation" warning banner. |
| **Quora** | Q&A platform with personal experiences. | Useful for complaint patterns; verify claims. |
| **CanadaVisa Forums** | Popular forum where applicants discuss timelines, approvals and strategies. | Good for DIY Express Entry guidance. |
| **Consultant Directories** | Online databases listing consultants or firms by location or specialty. | Some are paid listings; always cross-check with the CICC register. |
| **BBB**: Better Business Bureau | Private organization tracking business credibility and complaints. | One data point only; many firms are unrated. |
| **Justdial / MouthShut / consumercomplaints.in** ➕ Added | Indian review and complaint platforms. | Look for refund and "no response after payment" patterns. |
| **ratemyrcic.ca** ➕ Added | Independent review site for RCICs. | Extra cross-check for Canada-based consultants. |

## 9. India-Side Terms

|---|---|---|
| **MEA** ➕ Added | Ministry of External Affairs, Government of India. | Oversees overseas recruitment rules. |
| **eMigrate** ➕ Added | MEA's portal (emigrate.gov.in) listing registered recruiting agents and illegal agents. | Check any firm offering **job placement**. |
