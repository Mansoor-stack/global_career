# Immigration Consultant Vetting Checklist
### For a Respiratory Therapist moving from India to Canada

**Prepared for:** Mansoor Ahmed & family
**Compiled:** 28 October 2026
**Use it for:** any consultant, lawyer or agency, whether India-based or Canada-based, **before paying anything**.

> **How to use this:** Learn Part 1 before your first call. Ask the Part 2 questions on the call (a video call with the licensed person, not a salesperson). Score each firm in Part 5. Follow the Part 3 safety rules no matter how good the answers sound.

---

## Part 1: Know These Facts Before Any Call

If you know these, you can tell within minutes whether someone knows healthcare immigration or is just selling.

- [ ] Her occupation code is **NOC 32103** (Respiratory therapists). It is on the Express Entry **healthcare category** list and the new **high-wage occupation** list.
- [ ] She needs **12 months** of full-time experience for healthcare draws. She started 1 Sept 2026, so she's eligible **around September 2027**.
- [ ] **No Canadian licence is needed to receive an Invitation to Apply (ITA).** Licensing is a separate process run by provincial regulators, not consultants.
- [ ] The **CRTO** (Ontario) runs the RT competency assessment for Ontario, Nova Scotia, New Brunswick, Newfoundland & Labrador and Manitoba. It includes an **in-person Clinical Skills Assessment in Toronto**.
- [ ] From **3 November 2026**, a genuine job offer in a high-wage occupation is worth **50, 70 or 100 CRS points**, with **no LMIA needed**. This makes **fake job offers a growing scam**.
- [ ] Government fees: **CAD 990 processing + CAD 600 right-of-PR fee per adult**, plus biometrics (CAD 85/person, CAD 170 family max). Consultant fees are **separate**.
- [ ] Only these can legally charge you for Canadian immigration advice:
  - an **RCIC** (verify at **register.college-ic.ca**)
  - a **Canadian lawyer** (verify with the provincial law society)
  - a **Quebec notary**

  Indian "counsellors" or agents **cannot**.
- [ ] Normal fee structure: **flat fee in stages**, with a **written agreement signed before payment**. **No fees tied to approval** (contingency billing is not allowed).
- [ ] In India, **recruiting agents** must be registered on **emigrate.gov.in**. MEA rules cap their service charges at **₹20,000 per emigrant**.

## Part 2: Questions to Ask

Tick each box when you get a **reliable** answer.

### A. Identity & Licensing

| ✓ | Ask | Reliable answer | Red flag |
|---|---|---|---|
| [ ] | What is the full name and licence number of the person who will handle my file? | A name and R-number given immediately, with an invitation to verify | Vague: "our company is registered", "we have RCIC partners" |
| [ ] | Will that person be named on **form IMM 5476** and sign the service agreement? | "Yes, always." | "Our team handles it." / "Not needed." |
| [ ] | Can I speak with that licensed person **before** paying? | Yes, by video | Only salespeople or "counsellors" available |
| [ ] | Is your company itself licensed? | "Individuals are licensed, not companies. Here's who." | "We're IRCC-registered" / "ICCRC-approved" (neither is a real company licence) |
| [ ] | Have you ever been disciplined by the CICC or a law society? | A straight answer that matches the public register | Evasion or annoyance |
| [ ] | (Saskatchewan only) Are you licensed by the Government of Saskatchewan? | Yes, with proof | Doesn't know this is required |

### B. Expertise for Her Case

| ✓ | Ask | Reliable answer | Red flag |
|---|---|---|---|
| [ ] | What's the NOC code for respiratory therapists? | **32103** | Hesitation, or 32101 (that's LPNs) |
| [ ] | Does she need a Canadian licence before an Express Entry invitation? | "No, but start licensing in parallel." | "Yes, we'll get her licence first." |
| [ ] | Who assesses RTs for Nova Scotia? | **The CRTO** | Doesn't know, or names the wrong body |
| [ ] | How many respiratory therapist clients have you handled? | An honest number, even "none, but here's how we'd approach it" | "Hundreds," with no specifics |
| [ ] | What's her realistic CRS score, and is it competitive? | A calculated estimate with reasoning, including bad news | "Don't worry, we'll get it done." |
| [ ] | Which provinces suit an RT, and why? | Specific reasons (e.g., Ontario/CRTO, Saskatchewan Health Talent Pathway, Nova Scotia EE stream) | Generic list of "best provinces" |
| [ ] | How do the 3 Nov 2026 Express Entry changes affect us? | Explains job-offer points / Canadian experience accurately | Unaware of the changes |

### C. Fees & Contract

| ✓ | Ask | Reliable answer | Red flag |
|---|---|---|---|
| [ ] | Can I see the written service agreement **before** paying? | Yes, it's sent in advance | "Pay first, then we'll send it." |
| [ ] | What exactly is included, and what costs extra? | Itemized scope | "Complete package" with no detail |
| [ ] | Is payment by stage? | Stage 2 is paid **only if she's invited** | Large lump sum upfront |
| [ ] | What's the extra fee for my spouse? | Clear amount in writing | "We'll discuss later." |
| [ ] | What is your refund policy if I end the contract? | Written clause in the agreement | "Non-refundable," with no clause |
| [ ] | Is the consultation fee credited if I hire you? | Clear yes/no with a time limit | Unclear |
| [ ] | Which account do I pay into? | A **business account in the firm's name**, with an invoice | Personal account, cash, UPI to an individual |

### D. Transparency & Control

| ✓ | Ask | Reliable answer | Red flag |
|---|---|---|---|
| [ ] | Will I have access to my own IRCC account and all correspondence? | Yes, plus copies of everything submitted | "We manage everything; you don't need access." |
| [ ] | Can I review every form before submission? | Yes, required | "Just sign here." |
| [ ] | Who is my day-to-day contact, and how fast do you respond? | Named person + written response standard | Rotating case managers |
| [ ] | What happens if my case manager leaves? | Clear handover process; the licensed person stays responsible | No answer |

### E. Trap Questions (Honesty Tests)

An honest consultant **refuses the bait**.

| ✓ | Ask | Reliable answer | Red flag |
|---|---|---|---|
| [ ] | "Can you arrange a Canadian job offer letter for extra points?" | "No. Buying or arranging fake offers is misrepresentation and can ban you for 5 years." | "Yes, for an extra fee." |
| [ ] | "Can you guarantee PR?" | "No one can guarantee it; IRCC decides." | "100% success" / "full refund if refused" |
| [ ] | "Can you adjust her job duties so they fit better?" | "No. Duties must match what she actually does." | "We'll write the letter for you." |
| [ ] | "Can you speed up her CRTO licence?" | "No. That's between her and the regulator." | "We have contacts." |
| [ ] | "Do we even need full service?" | "Maybe not; a file review might be enough." | Pushes the most expensive package |

### F. India-Specific (for India-based firms)

| ✓ | Ask | Reliable answer | Red flag |
|---|---|---|---|
| [ ] | Is the licensed RCIC an employee, or a "partner" serving all your clients? | Clear answer; you meet them directly | One RCIC in Canada behind thousands of clients |
| [ ] | Do you offer job placement? If yes, what's your eMigrate recruiting-agent licence number? | Licence number, fees within the ₹20,000 cap, or "we don't do placement" | Lakhs of rupees for "job offers" |
| [ ] | Are your "counsellors" giving immigration advice? | "No, only the RCIC advises." | Salespeople giving eligibility advice |

## Part 3: Safety Rules (Regardless of Answers)

- [ ] **Verify independently.** Check the licence yourself on the CICC register, then contact the person through the details listed **there**, not the ones they gave you. Impersonation is common.
- [ ] **Never hand over control.** Don't give anyone your original passport or the only copy of documents. Never sign blank forms.
- [ ] **Check status yourself.** Confirm every IRCC update (invitation, approval) in **your own IRCC account**. Forged approval letters are a known scam.
- [ ] **Pay traceably.** Use a business account in the firm's name. Get an invoice/receipt for every payment.
- [ ] **Keep records.** Save agreements, receipts, emails and WhatsApp chats.
- [ ] **Never pay for a job offer.**
- [ ] **Read reviews the right way.** Sort Trustpilot, Google and Justdial by **lowest + newest**. Watch for refund-complaint patterns, firm-owned "complaints" sites, and "Top 10" listicles.
- [ ] **Know where to complain:**
  - CICC (consultants)
  - provincial law society (lawyers)
  - IRCC fraud webform
  - eMigrate / Protector General of Emigrants (Indian recruiting agents)
  - consumer court

## Part 4: The Lines That Should Give You Confidence

**The single most reassuring statement:**

> "I'm [name], RCIC R-[number]. Please verify me on the CICC register before you pay anything. I'll personally sign your agreement and be named as your representative."

**The second most reassuring thing is honest bad news:**

> "At one year of experience her score probably won't be competitive yet. And her licence is a separate process with the CRTO that I can't do for you, so start it yourselves now."

> ⚠️ **Anyone can memorize the right lines.** Real confidence comes when their **actions match their words**: you verify them on the register, the contract arrives before payment, and they turn down the job-offer trap.
