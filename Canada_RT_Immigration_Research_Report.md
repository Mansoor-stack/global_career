## 2. Executive Summary: The Key Findings

1. **Licensing is the real bottleneck, not the visa.** No immigration consultant can do licensing for her. Ontario's regulator (**CRTO**) runs the competency assessment for Ontario and also for Nova Scotia, Newfoundland & Labrador, New Brunswick and Manitoba. It ends with an **in-person Clinical Skills Assessment (CSA) in Toronto**.
2. **Earliest Express Entry eligibility is about September 2027.** Since 18 Feb 2026, the healthcare category needs **12 months** of full-time experience in the occupation. She started 1 Sept 2026, so she reaches 12 months around Sept 2027.
3. **NOC 32103 is on the Express Entry Healthcare & Social Services category list** and on the **new high-wage occupation list** (effective 3 Nov 2026). This matters for job-offer points.
4. **No consultant (Canada or India) has a verifiable respiratory-therapy-specific track record.** "Healthcare" marketing is almost all about nurses and physicians.
5. **Very few foreign-trained RTs have done this.** A Saskatchewan regulator report cited only **19** registered RTs in Canada (of ~8,000 at the time) who were originally educated outside Canada or the US. The data is old, but it shows the path is rarely taken.
6. **Best value strategy:** prepare the file yourselves. Pay a licensed consultant only for a strategy consultation or file review (about CAD 200–350) instead of full service (CAD 5,000–9,000 for a couple).
7. **The wife should be the principal applicant.** Only her experience unlocks healthcare draws. The STEM category (relevant to Mansoor) has been dormant for 30+ months.

## 4. Licensing: How a Foreign-Trained RT Gets Licensed in Canada

### 4.1 Who regulates RTs

| Province / Territory | Regulator / Credential | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Ontario | College of Respiratory Therapists of Ontario (**CRTO**) | Runs the competency assessment for several provinces |
| Alberta | College of Respiratory Therapists of Alberta (**CRTA**) | Own "Foreign Qualification Recognition" pathway |
| Saskatchewan, Manitoba (MARRT), New Brunswick (NBART), Nova Scotia (**NSRRT**, formerly NSCRT), Newfoundland & Labrador (NLCHP), PEI, Quebec (OPIQ, French) | Provincial regulators | MB, NB, NS, NL refer applicants to CRTO for assessment |
| British Columbia, Yukon, NWT, Nunavut | **Not regulated**; credential via **CSRT** (RRT) | BC society says IERTs can apply through CRTO |

### 4.2 CRTO process for internationally educated RTs

| Step | What happens | Cost (CAD) |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Online application | Opens a file and gives access to the applicant portal | **$75** |
| 2. Credential evaluation | **Course-by-course** report from WES, ICAS or ICES, sent directly to CRTO | Third-party fee |
| 3. Education review + structured interview | Detailed curriculum review (Education Program Review Worksheet with course references) + interview by 2 contracted RTs. Interview can be by video. | **$500** (covers both) |
| 4. Interim report | Meeting with CRTO staff to discuss gaps and next steps | — |
| 5. Clinical Skills Assessment (CSA) | Simulated clinical scenarios at a CRTO-approved facility **in Toronto**. Scheduling can take up to 3 months. | **$5,000** (raised from $4,250 in March 2024) |
| 6. Registration Committee decision | Graduate certificate may be issued | Registration fee |
| 7. National exam | **HPTC** (Health Professionals Testing Canada, formerly CBRC). In Nova Scotia there's an **18-month** window to pass. | Exam fee |

**Key facts:**
- Proof of PR or a work permit is needed **only before a licence is issued**, not to start the assessment. She can begin from India.
- **Gaps** are handled case by case. With **significant gaps**, the only option may be completing a full approved Canadian RT program. The old refresher/bridging "Pathways" were **discontinued**.
- Files inactive for **1 year are closed**, so keep moving.
- Some regulators (e.g., New Brunswick) waive the language test if the RT education and first language were solely English/French. **Check CRTO's Language Proficiency Requirements Policy.**
- New Brunswick: assessment alone may take up to **1 year**, plus exam timing.
- Manitoba (MARRT) estimate: licensing steps cost about **CAD 10,000**, excluding tuition for gap training. MARRT licence fee 2026–27: **$840/year**.
- CRTO's CSA scenarios include ICU care (e.g., post-extubation patient with ABG/NIPPV). A **neonatal & paediatric scenario** was added in 2024.

### 4.3 What she can do now at Apollo
- Build strong exposure in **mechanical ventilation, NIV, ABG interpretation, airway management, neonatal/paediatric care**.
- Collect **detailed syllabi, course outlines and clinical-hour records** from both BSc and MSc institutions now.
- Ask Apollo for an **employment letter matching NOC 32103 duties**.
- Aim for **2+ years** of acute-care experience. Some Canadian RT recruiters (e.g., IHR Canada) require **2 years of current acute-care experience** and a current licence in the country of residence.

## 5. Immigration Pathways

### 5.1 Express Entry: Federal Skilled Worker + Healthcare category (primary route)

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Occupation | NOC 32103 is on the **Healthcare & Social Services** category list |
| Experience needed | **12 months** full-time in the occupation within the last 3 years (raised from 6 months on 18 Feb 2026). Can be earned **abroad**. |
| Earliest eligibility | **~September 2027** |
| 2026 healthcare cut-offs | **467–475** CRS (e.g., 20 Feb 2026: 4,000 invitations at 467; **4 Sept 2026: 3,500 at 475**) |
| Draw frequency | Roughly every **65 days** recently; no fixed schedule |
| Tie-break | Earlier profile-creation time wins at the cut-off, so **create the profile as soon as eligible** |
| Licence needed for invitation? | **No.** Only NOC match + CRS matter at the invitation stage. |
| Principal applicant | **Wife.** Category draws require the principal applicant to qualify. |

### 5.2 Express Entry changes effective 3 November 2026
- A new **Labour Market Integration factor** adds points for **either** Canadian work experience **or** a qualifying job offer in a **high-wage occupation** (whichever is higher, never both).
- Job-offer points are back: **50 / 70 / 100** depending on the occupation's wage tier. **No LMIA needed.**
- **NOC 32103 is on the 186-occupation high-wage list.** The exact tier wasn't confirmed; check canada.ca.
- IRCC pauses invitations for up to **2 weeks** while it recalculates profiles.

### 5.3 Provincial Nominee Programs (a nomination adds +600 CRS)

| Province | Stream | Key requirement |
|---|---|---|
| Saskatchewan | **Health Talent Pathway** (SINP) | Job offer from a SK health employer; RTs are among the targeted occupations |
| Saskatchewan | **International Healthcare Worker EOI** (free) | Pre-application; SK health employers may contact you |
| Nova Scotia | **NS Express Entry stream** | **No job offer**; CLB 5 for TEER 2 jobs |
| Ontario | **Ontario Workforce Priority Stream** (launched June 2026; EOI opened 4 Aug 2026) | Ontario job offer required. A "Priority Healthcare" stream (no job offer) is planned but has **no launch date**. All 9 old OINP streams were repealed on 30 May 2026. |
| Alberta | **Dedicated Health Care Pathway** | Job offer + regulator proof. **RT not clearly on the published profession list; verify.** |
| BC | **Health Authority stream** | For public health-authority employees |

### 5.4 Other pathways
- **French:** French draws reached a low of **382** in 2026. Learning French to NCLC 7 is the single biggest CRS lever, but takes time.
- **Employer-sponsored work permit:** possible, but employers rarely hire before licensure progress.
- **Fallback:** if CRTO finds major gaps, complete a Canadian RT program (3 years; e.g., Michener, Conestoga, Fanshawe, TRU). That also leads to a post-graduation work permit.
- **Mansoor as principal?** Not recommended. The STEM category has been dormant 30+ months, and only the wife's experience unlocks healthcare draws. Mansoor should maximize **spouse points** (strong IELTS + credential assessment).

## 15. Red Flags & How to Verify a Consultant

### 15.1 Who can legally charge for Canadian immigration advice
Under **section 91 of Canada's Immigration and Refugee Protection Act**, only these may charge:
- **RCICs** in good standing with the **CICC** (College of Immigration and Citizenship Consultants)
- **Lawyers** in a Canadian provincial/territorial law society (and Ontario paralegals)
- **Quebec notaries**

Indian "counsellors" and agents are **not** authorized. IRCC may **return or refuse** applications involving unauthorized paid representatives. Misrepresentation can bring a **5-year ban**, and it falls on **you**, not the agent.

### 15.2 Caution signs
1. Guarantees of a visa, PR, job, or "100% success / 100% refund if rejected".
2. Refusal to give an RCIC number, or not being named on **form IMM 5476** (Use of a Representative). That's a "ghost consultant".
3. **Paid job offers / job-search / "resume marketing" packages.**
4. Suggestions to "adjust" job duties to fit a NOC code.
5. Cash-only payment, payment to personal accounts, no receipts.
6. Pressure to pay quickly; agreements shown only after payment.
7. Outdated "ICCRC" references (a sign of carelessness).
8. Claims they can "handle" licensing.
9. Firm-owned "complaints" websites, and "Top 10" listicles praising the firm.
10. Large chains where you never speak to the licensed person.

### 15.3 Verification checklist
- [ ] Get the consultant's **full name + RCIC number** (format R + 6 digits).
- [ ] Search **register.college-ic.ca**: status **Active**, "entitled to practise: Yes", no discipline.
- [ ] Contact them **only through the details on the register** (impersonation is common).
- [ ] For lawyers: check the **provincial law society directory** (e.g., Law Society of Ontario, Law Society of BC, Barreau du Québec).
- [ ] The **licensed person signs the service agreement** and appears on **IMM 5476**.
- [ ] Written agreement lists **scope, fees, milestones, and refund policy** before any payment.
- [ ] For a Saskatchewan application: the consultant/recruiter is on **Saskatchewan's licensed list**.
- [ ] **India-side (if anyone offers job placement):** check their **Recruiting Agent licence on emigrate.gov.in**. Under India's Emigration Act, 1983, overseas recruiters must be registered. MEA guidelines cap recruiting agents' service charges at **₹20,000 per emigrant**. As of 9 Apr 2026, MEA had notified **3,505 illegal agents**.
- [ ] Independent reviews: **ratemyrcic.ca**, Trustpilot (sort by lowest + newest), Google/Justdial (look for refund patterns).

**Where to complain:** CICC (consultants), the provincial law society (lawyers), IRCC fraud webform, India's **eMigrate / Protector General of Emigrants** (recruiting agents), consumer court.

### 15.4 Screening questions to ask any consultant
A genuine healthcare specialist should answer these instantly:
1. *Who runs the RT competency assessment for a Nova Scotia applicant?* → **CRTO**
2. *What's the NOC for respiratory therapists, and is it on both the healthcare category list and the new high-wage list?* → **32103; yes**
3. *Does she need a Canadian licence before an Express Entry invitation?* → **No**
